# CarTracking buggy mount

The primary assembly uses inboard rails. Its complete neutral footprint is **92.5 mm across the car × 90 mm from front to rear**. The camera, Benewake TFS20-L, two MG90S servos, pan fork and tilt cradle come directly from the previous gimbal STEP at their original scale.

## Check the fit on your car

1. Print **04_Rim_Fit_Gauge.stl** at 100% in millimetres. The two stepped jaws straddle the straight chassis sidewalls. Rest the gauge shoulders on the rim and try it at the middle and both ends of the intended mounting area. The gauge should settle over both walls without squeezing them.
2. The model assumes **92.5 mm across the outside of the tub** and approximately **87.7 mm between its inside faces**. The gauge has a 92.90 mm outside-wall opening and an 87.24 mm span across the two inner locating toes. A snug or uneven fit identifies the dimension to remeasure before printing the rails. The narrow gauge can also check the height of the actual wire loops.
3. Choose one pair of rails. The supplied assembly uses the **inboard pair**, which bonds to the inside walls and keeps the complete mount within the requested footprint. The **exterior option** bonds to the outside walls and spans **100.7 mm** over its feet. Both pairs take the same printed deck and upper gimbal. Each rail has a 6 mm high glue face below the rim. The forward cutaway in the left rail gives access to the stock power switch.
4. Dry-fit the rails with the deck locked in place to set their spacing. The recessed lock goes on the car’s right and the cable slot points toward the rear wing. Check full steering travel, cable slack and the switch on your particular car before bonding the two fixed rails.

## Install and remove

The four long slots accept the pedestal screws on **40 × 23 mm** centres. Fit the four M3 × 10 flat-head screws from below the deck and secure them with M3 hex nuts. The larger slot carries the sensor cables. The single recessed round hole takes an M3 × 10 button-head screw into the nut in the right rail.

The underside of the deck sits **30 mm above the chassis rim**. To remove the complete gimbal, take out the rail-lock screw, slide the deck **9 mm toward the front bumper**, and lift it clear of the four dovetail pockets. Leave slack in the leads or unplug them for removal. Use the same sequence in reverse to install it.

The stock car is an external reference for fitting the mount. Its uncovered tub, battery door, knobby tyres, suspension, dampers, steering, onboard electronics and wing follow the nine photographs. The assembly represents one ride height and steering position. The shelf and screw posts in a physical moulding, the wire routing and the clearances through suspension travel still need a hands-on check. The exported STEP contains named solid components in their assembled positions.
